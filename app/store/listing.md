## Google Play

**Short description** (80 or fewer; this one is 63):

> Chat with your own computer. It picks and runs the local model.

**Full description** (4000 or fewer):

> Simco SI is a private chat window for a computer you already run.
>
> You type everyday language. That computer turns it into the right prompt for the Granite model it chose for its own memory and graphics chip. It then turns the model's reply back into everyday language. The phone never downloads the model. The computer does.
>
> On first launch, a sea turtle screen asks for that computer's address. Simco SI then shows the computer detecting its own hardware, choosing Ollama, Granite, and Aider, and unlocking chat.
>
> What you get:
> • A messenger with a chat list and threads, built for phones and foldables
> • A live coral reef theme
> • Tools, Lifebook notes, and Advanced settings that stay on your computer
> • No public account, no ads, no tracking, and no rented cloud graphics card
>
> Your chats go only to the computer address you enter. Simco SI does not run a server of its own.
>
> Simco SI needs a computer running the Simco SI host. Without one, the app can only show the setup screen.
