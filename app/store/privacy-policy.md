# Simco SI Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Simco SI ("the app") is a chat client for a computer you run yourself. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

- Simco SI does not run its own servers. It has no Simco SI account system, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.
- Your messages go only to the computer address you type into the app.
- Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

## What the app stores on your phone

- **The computer address you enter**, so you do not have to type it again.
- **Display preferences**, such as the theme you picked.
- **Translator settings and a local translator history**, if you use the translator screen.

This data stays in the app's storage on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage, removes it.

## What the app sends, and where

When you use chat, sign in, or run setup, the app sends the following **only to the computer address you entered**:

- The text of your messages, and replies from that computer.
- Your email address and password, if you choose to sign in to that computer. Sign-in is optional.
- Setup requests, such as asking that computer to detect its hardware or install its local model.

That computer belongs to you, or to someone you chose to trust. What it stores, such as chat history, Lifebook notes, or its encrypted vault, is controlled on that computer, not by the app. To delete that data, delete it on that computer.

## Encryption in transit

If the address you enter starts with `https://`, traffic is encrypted. If it starts with `http://`, which is common on a home network, the traffic is **not** encrypted. Use `https://` whenever the computer is reachable over the internet.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera images, or device identifiers.
- It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
- It does not sell or share personal data.
- It does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Children

Simco SI is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

## Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data, such as crash reporting, this policy will be updated to name it **before** that version is released. The effective date above will change.
