Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · DRAFT awaiting owner approval

Simko SI ("we") runs the website simkosi.com and the Simko SI apps for iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a hashed password, or the account identifier Google or Apple gives us if you sign in with them.
  • Your content: the chats and messages you send and the replies you get, plus anything you choose to save: memories, routines, pictures you create and their prompts, shared report links, vault items (stored encrypted with your passphrase) and child profiles (a display name, character and age band, added only by a parent or guardian).
  • Voice: when you tap the microphone, the audio clip is sent for transcription and is not kept by us after the text comes back.
  • Usage: message and picture counts used for the free limit and billing, plus basic server and error logs (IP address, time, browser type) kept for security.
  • Payments: if you subscribe, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your subscription status and a Stripe customer ID, never your full card number.
  • Reports: if you report an AI reply, we keep the reply text, the reason and any note you add.

How we use it

  • To run the service: sign you in, keep your chats, answer your messages, and apply the free and paid limits.
  • To keep it safe: prevent abuse, fix errors, and review reported replies.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising. There are no third-party ads or ad trackers.

Who processes it

  • xAI (Grok): your messages, voice clips and picture prompts are sent to xAI's API to produce replies, speech and pictures.
  • Hostinger: hosts the website and database.
  • Stripe: processes payments if you subscribe.
  • Google / Apple: only if you choose to sign in with them.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (sign in, tap your email at the top, then Delete account) or by emailing support@simkosi.com from your account email. Deletion removes your account and all the content listed above. Billing records needed for tax and accounting are kept with your account number only.

Children

Simko SI is meant for adults. A parent or guardian may add child profiles under their own account after giving consent; children do not get their own accounts. If you believe a child gave us personal information without consent, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, vault items are encrypted, and private files are stored outside the public website.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Questions: support@simkosi.com.
