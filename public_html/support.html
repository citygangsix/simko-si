Support

Updated November 23, 2026

Need help with Simko SI? Email support@simkosi.com and we'll reply within two business days.

Common questions

  • I forgot my password. Tap Sign in, then Forgot password? and follow the emailed code.
  • A reply was wrong or offensive. Tap the flag on that reply and pick a reason. We review every report.
  • How do I cancel my plan? Open Usage, then Manage billing, and cancel. You keep access until the end of the paid month.

Delete your account

In the app or website: sign in, tap your email at the top, tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account, chats, memories, pictures, routines, vault items and child profiles are deleted right away.

Without the app: email support@simkosi.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Simko SI account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email. Billing records needed for tax are kept with your account number only.
