Help and FAQ

Quick answers about using Simko SI.

What is Simko SI?

A calm assistant for everyday life. You type or talk in plain English and Simko SI answers, using Grok models from xAI.

Who can use Grok chat right now?

Anyone can make a free account. Confirm your email and you can chat with Grok right away. During the preview these are demo accounts: free, with a daily Grok limit for each account.

What is DeepSearch?

DeepSearch looks things up on the live web and shows the sources it used, so you can check every fact.

What does Think do?

Think asks the model to reason step by step before it answers. It is slower and best for hard questions, plans and math.

Can I install Simko SI on my computer?

Yes. Choose Download for desktop on the home page or in the account menu. In Chrome or Edge it installs as an app with its own window. Safari users can choose File, Add to Dock. There are also small launchers for Windows, Mac and Linux on the download page.

Can I send photos or documents?

Yes. Tap the paper clip to attach up to 4 files per message: JPEG or PNG photos, PDFs, Word (.docx) or Excel (.xlsx) files, or text files.

How does voice work?

Tap the microphone to speak a message, or the voice button for a back-and-forth conversation. Replies can be read aloud; pick a voice and speed in voice settings.

What are Lifebook and routines?

Lifebook is a private journal of short daily notes. Routines are gentle reminders, such as a bedtime story or a homework nudge, delivered in the app and by email at the time you choose.

I forgot my password.

Choose Sign in, then Forgot password. We email a reset code to the address on your account. The code works for one hour.

Is there a daily limit?

Yes. To keep costs fair, each account has a daily Grok budget. If you reach it, Simko SI tells you, and the limit resets at midnight Eastern time.

How do I delete my account or download my data?

Open the account menu from your email at the top right. Delete account removes your account and everything tied to it. Download my data saves your chats as a file.

How is my data protected?

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, vault items are encrypted, and private files are stored outside the public website. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I report a bad answer?

Tap the flag next to a reply. Reports go to the owner for review.
