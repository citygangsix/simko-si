Simko SI

A calm assistant for everyday life

Ask in plain English and get a clear answer. Simko SI runs on Grok, so it can search the live web with sources, think a hard problem through step by step, read your photos and PDFs, and talk with you out loud.

  • Chat about money, health questions, school, work or a quiet evening plan.
  • DeepSearch the web and see where every fact came from.
  • Think mode for careful, step-by-step answers.
  • Voice: talk and listen, hands free.
  • Lifebook and routines for gentle daily check-ins.
  • Connectors for Google Drive files you pick and Google Calendar events you approve.

Make a free account, confirm your email, and chat with Grok. Demo account: free during preview, with a daily limit.

              Files

                Read a page

                Simko SI can fetch one page from an allowed site and read the words. Links you paste into chat are read the same way. It does not log in, and it does not drive a browser. That needs a computer, not shared hosting.

                Allowed sites (owner)

                One site per line. Subdomains are included. Pasted links to other sites are not opened. Put * on its own line to allow any public page.

                Phone call

                Phone calls are not connected. They cost money per minute, so this stays off until a provider is chosen.

                Template and team

                Share an agent setup with a link. Invite a co-parent or a teammate.

                  Agents side by side
