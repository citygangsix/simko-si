## Full description (max 4000)
Simko SI is a calm assistant for everyday life. Ask in plain English and get a clear answer.

• Chat about money, health questions, school, work or a quiet evening plan
• DeepSearch looks things up on the live web and shows every source
• Think mode works hard problems through step by step
• Send photos, PDFs, Word or Excel files and ask about them
• Voice: tap the mic to talk, and hear replies read aloud in a choice of voices
• Pictures: describe a picture and Simko SI makes it, or edit one you already have
• Lifebook and routines: short daily notes and gentle reminders at the time you pick
• Reply language: write in any language and get answers in yours
• Download your data or delete your account at any time from the account menu

Simko SI runs on Grok models from xAI. AI replies can be wrong, so check anything important. Tap the flag on any reply to report it.

Simko SI is in early access. Make a free account to save your place.
