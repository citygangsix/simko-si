## App Review notes (draft)
Simko SI is an AI assistant. Sign in with the demo account below (email + password).
- Chat: tap New chat (+), type a question, tap Send. DeepSearch and Think are toggles under the message box. Mic = voice input; the speaker on a reply reads it aloud.
- Native features: offline screen with automatic reconnect, haptics on send/mic, native share sheet, push notifications for routine reminders (once enabled), universal links for simkosi.com.
- Delete account: tap your email at the top > Delete account > type DELETE > Delete my account.
- Report an AI reply: tap the flag on the reply.
Demo account: **review@simkosi.com** (password given to Apple only in the App Review Information form, never in chat or git). The account has Grok enabled and no daily limit during review.
